A scenario.
Not a crystal ball.

All 758 rows come from the sealed input. No live requests, price feeds, invented eligibility or protocol-specific URLs.

The conditional market-cap model

TVL × peer multiple × growth × listing-age proxy

  1. Use positive circulating market-cap/TVL ratios, deduplicated by CoinGecko ID or symbol (largest-TVL record); exclude CEX. Clip ratios to 0.02–20×.
  2. Geometric shrinkage: category median weighted n/(n+6), global median weighted 6/(n+6). Zero peers use the global median.
  3. Growth = 1 + 0.35 × winsorized seven-day TVL change / 100. Winsor limits are the frozen universe's 10th/90th percentiles, bounded to −50% / +100%.
  4. Listing proxy = 0.8 + 0.2 × min(days tracked,1095)/1095. Missing date uses 365 days; missing growth is neutral.

The low–high envelope is a sensitivity range, not a probability interval: category ratio IQR factor, floored at 2.5× (4× for sparse peers), capped at 8×, widened for missing age/growth. No row is high confidence. Token supply is unknown, so no price or FDV is inferred. Rows with a token recorded retain an explicitly hypothetical model value, separate from observed market cap; they are not pre-token opportunities.

Watchlist membership is authoritative input, not our token verification. Other versioned, staking and bridge products are held in “Verify product.” No parent links can be inferred from these ten fields. TVL may be double-counted; ratios across categories are not economic equivalents.
